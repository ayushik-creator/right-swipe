Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Bird Media (“Bird Media,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit BirdMedia.io (the “Website”) or contact us through the Website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to browse our Website.

If you choose to contact us through the Website, we may collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Your email address
  • Your name, if provided
  • Your company name, if provided
  • Any information you include in your message or inquiry

We ask that you do not submit sensitive personal information through our Website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Follow up regarding potential business opportunities
  • Maintain and improve our Website and services
  • Protect the security and integrity of our Website
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Website and Technical Information

Like most websites, our hosting and technology providers may automatically process limited technical information when you access the Website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and basic request or server-log data.

This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the Website.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Bird Media does not use the Website to intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

Our Website or the third-party services used to host and operate it may use essential cookies or similar technologies necessary for security, functionality, and performance.

If we introduce analytics, advertising cookies, pixels, or similar tracking technologies in the future, we may update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate our Website or communicate with you, such as website hosting, infrastructure, or email providers. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect our rights or users, or prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats.

6. Data Retention

We retain information you provide for only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain appropriate business records, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect the information we receive.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those third parties.

We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us if you would like to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information you have provided to us.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights regarding your personal information. We will respond to valid requests as required by applicable law.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our Website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Website. If we become aware that such information has been provided to us, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.
